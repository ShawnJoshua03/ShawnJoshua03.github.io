I joined the remote monitoring team for the Toronto and Waxahachie plants. Day to day that meant report checks and data visualization; the larger piece of work was six months of plant history, which I used to set upper and lower limit thresholds that proved 98% accurate. Those thresholds caught a 15% anomaly in glass heat levels, which led to a multi-system reorganization and new data solutions.

With Power BI, Seeq, TwinThread, Python and Excel, I helped redevelop the code that automates real-time, graph-based anomaly detection. It reached a 99.3% success rate and helped reduce downtime.

For the Memphis and Medina plants I ran cloud-based anomaly detection analysis and refined the sheet position radius. That led to better allocation of skilled labor and a 10% improvement in operational efficiency.
