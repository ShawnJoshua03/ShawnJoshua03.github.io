I built Power BI dashboards for IT incident and outage data, combining structured logs with freeform entries. They made Time to Detect, Time to Resolve and root-cause trends visible by team and application. More than 50 IT staff adopted them, and outage detection analysis improved by 25%.

On the finance side, I designed and optimized the Coupa and Oracle reporting spaces used for spend comparisons across more than $80M in vendor spend. That meant better layouts, refined filters, and accurate calculations for location-specific time off among offshore contractors. Reporting became more efficient for more than 30 finance managers.

I also evaluated Power Automate for moving Coupa data into Power BI without manual steps, which laid the groundwork for repeatable, automated reporting cycles. I built documentation frameworks for tracking multi-year Statement of Work costs, and ran offshore desktop inventory reporting with 10 global vendors: validating and cleaning their Excel submissions and consolidating more than 150 line items a month into pivot-based summaries for leadership.

I also learned to write the same finding two ways: once for executive stakeholders, once for the operational teams who run the systems.
